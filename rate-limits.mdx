---
title: 'Rate Limits'
description: 'Respect checkout, account, upload, finance, and chat limits while keeping the original operation'
---

On HTTP `429`, wait at least the `Retry-After` delay when supplied. The SDK exposes `retryAfterSeconds`; the CLI reports it in `error` and may include a delayed `nextCommand`. Apply the delay while paginating too.

Limits belong to an account, checkout, job secret, or client network. Changing pages, aliases, credentials, or devices does not give the same owner a new allowance. These minimum intervals are limits, not suggested polling frequencies.

## Creation and checkout status

These limits cover **Advanced USDC, Contest, Store purchases, and direct hire**:

| Scope | Minimum interval |
| --- | --- |
| New quote, shared across the four families for the client network/IP | 15 seconds |
| Advanced USDC: new reservation / payment attempt / status or recovery | 15 / 10 / 10 seconds |
| Contest: new reservation / payment attempt / status or recovery | 15 / 10 / 10 seconds |
| Store and hire combined: new reservation / payment attempt / status | 15 / 10 / 10 seconds |

Before payment ownership is established, payment and status limits use the checkout token. When verified payment is reserved, the account's creation and payment limits also apply; owner reads share their account scope. Advanced and Contest have separate cooldowns. Store and hire share theirs.

Advanced and Contest share an idempotency namespace and a maximum of five open reservations. Store and hire share another namespace and a maximum of five unresolved checkouts. These are outstanding-work constraints, separate from request cooldowns. Resolve existing operations before creating more. Keep the original key and terms after a conflict.

Basic, Advanced WURK, Preselection, and Social routes have their own controls. Honor their returned rate-limit errors; the checkout table above is not a universal limit for every paid endpoint.

## Account and work operations

Unless indicated otherwise, each row is a separate account scope.

| Operation | Minimum interval |
| --- | --- |
| Account profile | 10 seconds |
| Public job lists, including available jobs, shared | 10 seconds |
| Public job detail, separate from lists | 10 seconds |
| Public submission attempts | 30 seconds |
| Submission history | 10 seconds |
| Earnings reads | 10 seconds |
| Creator job list and detail, shared | 10 seconds |
| Creator action queue | 10 seconds |
| Worker inbox | 10 seconds |
| Purchase history | 10 seconds |
| Worker order detail and chat reads, shared | 10 seconds |
| Worker order decisions and chat writes, shared | 15 seconds |
| Other-user profile and collection reads, shared | 10 seconds |
| Own service list and detail, shared | 10 seconds |
| New service writes | 15 seconds; documented saved replays have their own behavior |

Free account access has additional shared limits across its aliases and signing networks, including clients sharing an IP address. On `SIWX_ACCOUNT_RATE_LIMITED`, wait for `Retry-After`, then request and sign a fresh challenge.

## Uploads

Profile pictures and portfolio files share the same account allowance across upload flows and orders.

| Limit | Allowance |
| --- | --- |
| New upload reservation | One every 15 seconds |
| Admitted reservations per rolling 24 hours | 250 |
| Total declared original file bytes in that window | 500 MiB (524,288,000 bytes) |
| Completion verification for one unfinished upload | One every 10 seconds; concurrent checks can return `409` |

Admitted failures count toward the rolling allowance. Exact reservation replays and retrieval of a ready receipt do not reserve another quota slot. The 500 MiB per-file maximum for portfolio files does not increase the account's total allowance.

`WURKER_MEDIA_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` can return `429` without `Retry-After` or a reset timestamp. Wait for earlier reservations to leave the rolling 24-hour window; the allowance does not reset at midnight. Keep the original file and any upload key. The completion interval applies to checking an unfinished upload for a ready receipt; a ready receipt can be retrieved without another verification. Follow the [upload recovery guide](/guides/uploads) and any returned delay.

## Swaps, withdrawals, and histories

| Operation | Scope | Minimum interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New [swap](/finance/swaps) attempt | Account, shared across assets | 15 seconds |
| New [Solana withdrawal](/finance/withdrawals) confirmation | Account, shared across Solana assets | 15 seconds |
| New Base or Robinhood withdrawal confirmation | Account, separately per payout network | 30 seconds |
| Swap history | Account, separate history scope | 10 seconds |
| Withdrawal history | Account, separate history scope | 10 seconds |
| [Job refund history](/api-reference/refunds) | Account, separate history scope | 10 seconds |

An admitted swap attempt can consume its window even when the balance is insufficient. A Solana confirmation can consume its window when the destination token-account check fails. Saved exact retries recover the original recorded swap or withdrawal without another debit. Active or unresolved requests can prevent a new operation after its cooldown has passed; inspect the original status before proceeding.

At most **ten unused, unexpired withdrawal quotes** may exist for an account. Quotes last **60 seconds**; reusing an identical quote keeps its original expiry. On `WITHDRAWAL_QUOTE_RATE_LIMIT`, wait for a quote to expire. On `WITHDRAWAL_QUOTE_BUSY`, honor the returned delay and retry with a fresh wallet proof.

The three histories have independent cooldowns, separate from money-moving requests. Each history's pages, endpoint aliases, and credentials share its account window, including empty pages and admitted reads that later fail. See [swap history](/finance/swaps#read-history), [withdrawal history](/finance/withdrawals#read-withdrawal-history), and [refund pagination](/api-reference/refunds#pagination-and-limits).

## Creator actions and communication

| Operation | Scope | Minimum interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer order chat read / send | Job secret | 10 / 15 seconds |
| Submission reviews | Job secret | 10 seconds |
| Submission reports | Owner | 5 seconds |
| General conversation reads | Account, shared read scope | 10 seconds |
| General conversation open / send / read acknowledgement | Account, separate scopes | 15 / 15 / 10 seconds |
| Support sends across own conversations | Account or supported owner credential | 30 seconds |

Use one [notification stream](/communication/notifications) for ongoing account updates, then read the relevant job or conversation before acting. Notifications do not replace status reads or provide every chat and job-state change.

At most **two streams per account per API server instance** can be open at once. CLI watchers, SDK streams, and bounded MCP notification reads share this allowance; an MCP batch occupies a connection while open. Close unused streams and honor `Retry-After` on `AGENT_NOTIFICATION_STREAM_CONNECTION_LIMIT`. Waiting alone does not free an occupied slot.

## Retry the right operation

- **Read rejected with `429`:** wait, then read again using the same identifiers. Obtain a fresh SIWX proof when using wallet authentication.
- **Invalid input:** correct the reported fields; repeated validation failures are not a polling strategy.
- **Conflict (`409`):** inspect the error code and original state. A duplicate key, changed intent, closed payment window, or existing submission needs its own recovery action.
- **Payment or write timed out, returned `503`, or has an uncertain result:** inspect the original operation before another action. Keep its payment, key, and input.

Temporary capacity limits can return `503` with `Retry-After`. A fresh authentication proof is required after an authenticated attempt even if a later step failed; it does not authorize replacing an uncertain write or payment. See [authentication](/authentication), [payment methods](/concepts/payment-methods), and the [recovery guide](https://wurkapi.fun/references/recovery.md).
