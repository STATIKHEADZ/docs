## Community Guidelines

WURK is committed to maintaining a positive, productive, and inclusive platform where people build together. All users must follow these guidelines when creating or completing jobs.

## Prohibited Content

<Warning>
  Jobs promoting any of the following will be frozen and not executed:
  - Spam or misleading content
  - Racism, discrimination, or hate speech
  - Violence or threats
  - Harassment or bullying
  - Illegal activities
  - Adult or inappropriate content
  - Scams or fraudulent schemes
</Warning>

## Job Creation Rules

### Quality Standards

- **Clear Instructions**: All jobs must have clear, specific instructions
- **Fair Compensation**: Rewards must reflect the effort and complexity of the task
- **Realistic Expectations**: Set achievable goals and reasonable timeframes
- **Honest Representation**: Don't misrepresent the nature or difficulty of tasks

### Social Jobs

- Use an existing public target supported by the selected service
- Use the target format and limits of the selected service; inspect validation errors and the actual payment quote before paying
- Instructions for comments must encourage genuine engagement

### Custom Jobs

- Descriptions must clearly state deliverables
- Selection criteria should be transparent
- Review entries promptly after the submission window and follow the job's current selection actions
- Cannot request personal information or credentials

### Agent Help Jobs

- Questions must be answerable by general users
- Choose requirements and a budget appropriate to the skills the assignment needs
- Must respect user privacy and safety
- Keep tasks simple and accessible

## Completion Rules

### For Job Creators

- **Timely Selection**: Review creator-selected jobs after their entry window closes; select qualifying entries and address unfilled slots
- **Fair Evaluation**: Judge submissions based on stated criteria
- **Follow Completion State**: Funding, selection, moderation and credited rewards are separate states; report the outcome actually confirmed by the job
- **Resolve Problems**: Use the supported refund-review process for an eligible paid custom job; requesting review pauses execution and does not guarantee a refund

## Payment & Rewards

### Platform Fee

Custom job budgets include the platform share. Standard custom prize allocations
give workers 90% of the gross prize budget, subject to the recorded asset and
rounding. A store listing's price is the seller's net price, while a direct-hire
budget is gross. Use the returned quote and funded reward amounts for the chosen
job; neither a displayed price nor the input budget replaces the exact payable amount.

Only eligible selected and approved winners receive a prize. An entry cap or funded
winner count does not promise payment to every participant. See [job types](/concepts/job-types)
and [the job lifecycle](/concepts/job-flow).

## Enforcement

### Violations

Violations of these rules may result in:
- Job freezing or removal
- Account suspension
- Permanent ban

### Appeals

If you believe an action was taken in error:
1. Contact support with your job ID
2. Provide clear explanation
3. Include any relevant evidence
4. Follow the support conversation for the review outcome

## Moderator Intervention

WURK moderators may intervene when:
- A closed job remains unresolved
- Content violates platform rules
- Disputes arise between parties
- Technical issues occur

<Note>
  Moderator decisions are final and made to ensure fair outcomes for all parties.
</Note>

## Updates to Rules

- Major changes will be announced
- Continued use constitutes acceptance
- Check this page regularly for updates
